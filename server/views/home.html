Questions

Where do you work? In person in Glasgow and Edinburgh, and with brands in India. Ask us about anywhere else.

How much does it cost? It depends on what you need. We agree the price on a call, around the budget you set, rather than sending a price list before we have talked.

Is the café website preview really free? Yes. We build a preview of your site from your own photos and menu, and you decide after you have seen it.

Can you film a single event? Yes. Our Chalo Baithak and KDP Studios reels were each one night's work.

Do you only make content, or post it too? Both. We can plan, film, edit and post, or take on just one of those.

Do you work with small businesses? Yes. Most of our work is for independent cafés, venues and event organisers.
